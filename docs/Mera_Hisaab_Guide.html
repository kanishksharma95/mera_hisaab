1. What is Mera Hisaab?

A simple, mobile-first business-management (ERP) web app for Indian local shops. One shopkeeper can manage products, sales/billing, purchases, stock, customers, udhaar (credit), suppliers, and profit — all from one place. It is a multi-tenant SaaS: one application safely serves thousands of independent shops, and each shop's data is completely isolated from every other shop.

Everything is dynamic and database-driven — no hardcoded shops, products, prices, categories, payment methods, or dashboard numbers. Every figure you see is calculated live from the database.

3. How to run the app

  1. Open a terminal in the project folder C:\Users\Kanishak\Desktop\mera_hisaab.
  2. Create an admin login (one time): .\venv\Scripts\python.exe manage.py createsuperuser
  3. Start the server: .\venv\Scripts\python.exe manage.py runserver
  4. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000/ in your browser.
Database: The app currently uses a SQLite file, db.sqlite3, in the project folder (created automatically). To switch to PostgreSQL, set USE_SQLITE=0 and the POSTGRES_* values in the .env file, then run migrate. View data anytime via the Admin panel, or open db.sqlite3 in DBeaver (New Connection → SQLite → pick the file).

4. First-time flow

Register → Create your business (onboarding) → Pick business type → Dashboard → Add products → Add customers → Purchase stock → Make sales. Stock, udhaar, and profit all update automatically.

5. Feature guide (how to use each part)

Login & business

Log in with your email and password. You can own or belong to several shops; switch between them from the business dropdown in the top bar. Toggle dark mode with the moon icon.

Dashboard

Live cards: Today's Sales, Purchases, Expenses, Profit, Customers, Pending Udhaar, Products, Low Stock. Use the Period filter (Today / Week / Month / Custom, etc.) to change the range. All numbers are real, from your data.

Products & Categories

  1. Products → Add Product: name, SKU, barcode, prices (purchase & selling), tax %, unit, minimum stock, image, and an opening stock.
  2. Extra fields appear automatically based on your business type (e.g. a Mobile shop sees IMEI/Model; Garments sees Size/Color).
  3. Categories: group products (e.g. Rice, Snacks). Products and categories can be deactivated (soft-delete), never lost.

Inventory

An overview of stock on hand, minimum level, and stock value. The Low stock button filters items at/below their minimum so you know what to reorder. Use Adjust to correct a quantity (breakage, counting) — it records an adjustment in the item's history. Normally stock moves by itself: purchases increase it, sales decrease it.

Customers & Udhaar (Khata)

  1. Customers → Add Customer (name, phone, credit limit, notes).
  2. Each customer has a ledger: a positive balance means they owe you (udhaar).
  3. Add udhaar increases their balance; Record payment reduces it. Over-payment becomes an advance.
  4. Udhaar page: all customers who owe money, most-owed first, with total outstanding and one-click Collect.

POS / Sales (billing)

  1. Open POS / Sales (or the Sell tab on mobile). Search or scan a product and tap to add it to the cart.
  2. Adjust quantity and discount. Choose a customer (needed if any amount stays on udhaar) and a payment method.
  3. Click Complete Sale. The app then, in one safe step: creates the invoice, reduces stock, records the payment, puts any unpaid balance on the customer's udhaar, and calculates profit.
  4. On the invoice you can Print, download PDF, or Share on WhatsApp (a link with the invoice summary and a public view page, ending with “Powered by Mera Hisaab”).
All money is calculated on the server (never trusted from the browser), using exact decimals. Invoice numbers like MH-2026-000001 are generated per shop, in order, with no duplicates.

Suppliers & Purchases

  1. Suppliers → Add Supplier. Each supplier has a payable ledger (what you owe them).
  2. Purchases → New Purchase: add products with quantity and cost, choose the supplier and how much you paid now.
  3. On save: stock increases automatically, the product's cost is updated (for accurate profit), and any unpaid amount becomes supplier payable. Pay it later from the supplier's page.

Payment methods

Each shop gets an editable set (Cash, UPI, Card, Bank Transfer, Credit/Udhaar, Other). Choosing Credit/Udhaar sends the amount to the customer's khata instead of counting it as received. These are stored in the database, not hardcoded.

Super Admin panel

At /admin/ (needs a superuser account). This is the platform-owner view: manage shops, users, business types & their dynamic fields, and inspect all records. Money fields are read-only there on purpose — they change only through the app's logic.

6. Security & correctness

  • Tenant isolation: every record belongs to a business; the active shop comes from your logged-in session, never from a browser-supplied id. Automated tests confirm one shop cannot see or touch another shop's data.
  • Money: stored as exact decimals (never floating point); all totals, tax, discounts and balances computed on the backend.
  • Consistency: sales, purchases, and ledger updates run inside atomic, row-locked transactions — they either complete fully or roll back; stock and balances never drift.
  • CSRF protection, secure password hashing, and Django's ORM (no raw SQL) are used throughout.

8. Known limitations (today)

  • No sale/purchase return or cancel yet (documents are immutable once posted).
  • GST is a single tax rate per product; full CGST/SGST/IGST + HSN is Phase 7.
  • WhatsApp shares a link (not an attached PDF file) — a platform limitation without the WhatsApp Business API.
  • Roles are shop-level (owner/staff) for now; fine-grained permissions come in Phase 8.
  • Expenses and true net profit are Phase 6 (dashboard Profit currently shows gross profit from sales).
